About Austin

Austin Hatch grew up in Fort Wayne, Indiana. In 2003, at age 8, he survived a plane crash that killed his mother, sister and brother. His father later remarried, and the blended family gave him a sense of normal life again. As a sophomore he led Canterbury High School to its first sectional title and was named The News-Sentinel's Prep Player of the Year. On June 15, 2011, Michigan's John Beilein offered him a full scholarship to his mother's alma mater. Nine days later a second crash, in Charlevoix, Michigan, killed his father and stepmother and left him in a medically induced coma for about two months. He relearned how to walk, talk and eat, signed with Michigan in 2013, scored his only college point on a free throw in December 2014 and spent two more seasons as an undergraduate assistant coach. After graduating he worked in Domino's corporate office, then built the G.R.I.T. framework he now teaches to organizations across the country. He lives in Michigan with his wife, Abby, a former All-America volleyball player at Michigan, and their two children.
