About Mike

Mike Lemaire was born and raised in Paris, France, and started judo shortly after his fourth birthday, adding karate and kickboxing at 12. He moved to the United States at 19 and, in San Diego, turned to Muay Thai. Over about a decade as a professional he won the WBC and WCK Muay Thai world titles, fought for Lion Fight and World Cup Kickboxing, and in 2013 won the Road to GLORY middleweight tournament, stopping two of three opponents and beating favorite Eddie Walker in the final to earn a GLORY Kickboxing contract. He went on to compete eight times in GLORY. Between fights he became a head coach and personal trainer, and today leads the Victory MMA fight team in San Diego. Drawing on holistic wellness, ancestral nutrition and training with top-level professionals, he founded Fitness Mike Lemaire and its Biological Recode System™, coaching executives and busy men on training, nutrition, recovery and discipline.
