About Zion

Zion Clark was born on September 29, 1997, in Columbus, Ohio, without legs due to caudal regression syndrome. Given up at birth, he grew up moving between foster homes before being adopted. He found wrestling, and music as his church's lead drummer, at a young age, then went several seasons without a win. Under coach Gil Donahue at Massillon Washington High School he finished his senior year 33–15, losing 1–0 in sudden death one match short of the Ohio state championships, and won a state wheelchair racing title. He went on to wrestle and race at Kent State University's Tuscarawas campus. His story became ZION, a 2018 Sundance selection on Netflix that won two Sports Emmys. He holds three Guinness World Records, won his professional MMA debut in December 2022, reached the semifinals of America's Got Talent in 2023 and has written two books with journalist James S. Hirsch.
