At a glance

Garin Heslop is the co-founder and CEO of GYNERGY, a San Diego company and AI operating system for human, leadership and business performance. A three-time founder, he became a self-made millionaire in real estate by 23, then built Medcare Farms into a $14M-a-year operation with more than 100 employees. About Garin

Garin Heslop is the co-founder and CEO of GYNERGY, built on a five-pillar framework of health, relationships, mindset, wealth and legacy. He entered real estate at 19 and was a self-made millionaire by 23, running a company across land development, new construction and residential resale until a near-fatal motorcycle accident at 25. He then built Medcare Farms, a vertically integrated California operation that grew to more than $14M a year and 100+ employees. In 2022 an eight-month stretch took his father, his grandmother, his marriage and his business. He rebuilt, co-founded GYNERGY with Yesi Magaña in 2024, launched the GYNERGY.AI platform and its ARIA AI coach in 2025, and has coached more than 200 CEOs, founders and athletes.
