About Beaver

Beaver Fleming started skating at 10 in Knoxville, Tennessee, on an 8-foot halfpipe his parents built in the backyard. At 17 he moved to California to finish high school at Woodward West, where Travis Pastrana saw him skate and invited him to join Nitro Circus. He flew to Australia on his 18th birthday and has since toured the world as an official Nitro Circus cast member, appeared in the Action Figures films and created multiple world-first tricks. With a heart to see others live their dreams, he speaks at schools and youth groups about determination and perseverance. Based in San Diego, the action sports capital of the world, he trains at local skateparks and partners with local organizations on camps and lessons for the next generation. A husband and father, he brings his wife Gabrielle and son Oliver Knox on the road as much as possible.
